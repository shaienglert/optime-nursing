## Decision Psychology Research Coverage
- Fear of moving: represented in fear catalog and transition-related concern sets.
- Fear of loneliness: represented in loneliness-focused entries and social concern categories.
- Fear of losing independence: represented in identity/autonomy concern sets.
- Family guilt: represented in family-category concerns and blame/guilt entries.
- Decision delay: represented indirectly through uncertainty and unresolved-tradeoff calibration notes.
- Transition anxiety: represented in transition-category concern sets and failed-transition causes.
- Adaptation: represented in top-50 successful adaptation factors.
- Successful transitions: represented in outcome validation success metrics and adaptation factors.
- Failed transitions: represented in top-50 failed transition causes.
- Regret and satisfaction: represented through user feedback, satisfaction metrics, and narrative calibration notes in outcome validation reporting.
