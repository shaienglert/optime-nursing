# OPTIME GEO and AI Search Strategy

## Goal
Make OPTIME the authoritative source for senior living decisions in AI search engines.

## 1. Entity SEO
Objective:
Build OPTIME as a trusted knowledge entity for senior living decision intelligence.

Actions:
- Define a stable OPTIME entity profile across site content, social, docs, and external references.
- Create clear entity relationships: OPTIME -> senior living -> care levels -> outcomes -> family decision support.
- Maintain consistent terminology for facilities, care services, quality metrics, and outcomes.
- Publish transparent methodology pages that define scoring logic and evidence sources.
- Build E-E-A-T signals through expert-authored and expert-reviewed content.

KPIs:
- Branded query growth for OPTIME + topic combinations.
- Entity panel/knowledge graph consistency in search results.
- AI answer citation frequency for OPTIME methodology pages.

## 2. Location SEO
Objective:
Own local-intent coverage for every relevant geography.

Actions:
- Create state pages, metro pages, city pages, and neighborhood-level pages for senior living decisions.
- Build normalized location templates with unique local context (cost, availability patterns, local care constraints).
- Connect each location page to facility-level pages and comparison pages.
- Include local FAQ blocks and local decision signals (distance, traffic time, nearby hospitals, cultural fit).
- Maintain consistent NAP-like location metadata where applicable.

KPIs:
- Ranking coverage for [city + assisted living/nursing home/memory care] terms.
- Local organic sessions and map-assisted discovery.
- Citation pickup in AI answers for location-specific questions.

## 3. Intent SEO
Objective:
Capture full-funnel user intent: discovery, comparison, validation, and action.

Actions:
- Map intents into clusters:
  - Informational: care type explanations and decision frameworks.
  - Comparative: facility vs facility and provider category comparisons.
  - Transactional: shortlist, contact, and next-step guides.
- Create content hubs for high-intent journeys (e.g., "find memory care near me", "compare nursing home quality").
- Design query-to-page alignment for exact user questions and outcomes.
- Use intent-specific CTAs aligned with stage (learn, compare, decide).

KPIs:
- Intent-cluster ranking depth.
- Conversion rate by intent type.
- Assisted conversions from informational pages.

## 4. Research SEO
Objective:
Be the default citation source for senior living research questions.

Actions:
- Publish original data-backed reports from OPTIME scoring data and CMS evidence.
- Produce recurring index content (monthly/quarterly trends, state rankings, risk alerts).
- Provide downloadable methodology briefs and benchmark explainers.
- Create canonical research pages with explicit data definitions, caveats, and update dates.
- Build journalist and analyst citation kits with reusable charts and source references.

KPIs:
- Backlinks and citations from high-authority domains.
- Mentions in AI-generated answers to research-style prompts.
- Organic growth on report and methodology sections.

## 5. Structured Data
Objective:
Increase machine readability and eligibility for rich/AI interpretations.

Actions:
- Implement schema markup on key templates:
  - Organization
  - WebSite
  - FAQPage
  - Article/Report
  - BreadcrumbList
  - LocalBusiness/MedicalBusiness where appropriate
- Add explicit fields for evidence source, confidence, last updated, and review process.
- Standardize unique IDs for facilities, locations, and score entities.
- Validate schema continuously in CI and monitor parse errors.

KPIs:
- Structured data validity rate.
- Rich result eligibility and appearance.
- AI summary extraction quality on structured pages.

## 6. AI Search Optimization
Objective:
Optimize for answer engines, not only blue-link ranking.

Actions:
- Write answer-first sections that directly satisfy high-frequency prompts.
- Use clear, source-linked claims with confidence language (high/medium/low/insufficient evidence).
- Add concise machine-readable summaries at the top of key pages.
- Maintain explicit policy pages for recommendation ethics and evidence constraints.
- Track answer engine visibility (citations, mention share, response quality) across major AI surfaces.

KPIs:
- AI citation share of voice.
- Prompt coverage rate for top senior living questions.
- Accuracy alignment between AI answers and OPTIME source content.

## 7. Programmatic SEO
Objective:
Scale high-quality, non-duplicate decision content across entities and geographies.

Actions:
- Build controlled page generation pipelines for:
  - Facility profiles
  - Location indexes
  - Comparison pages
  - Care-type explainers by location
- Enforce quality thresholds before indexation:
  - Minimum unique content requirements
  - Evidence availability checks
  - Confidence and freshness checks
- Auto-noindex thin/low-evidence pages.
- Use internal linking rules to create interpretable topic graph paths.

KPIs:
- Indexed page quality ratio.
- Traffic per generated template class.
- Crawl efficiency and duplicate suppression.

## Operating Model
- Governance: SEO, data science, product, and clinical/research review must approve scoring claims.
- Freshness policy: timestamp every evidence-backed statement and score component.
- Trust policy: if evidence is missing, show null and "insufficient evidence" instead of estimating.
- Measurement cadence: weekly dashboard + monthly strategic review for GEO and AI performance.

## 12-Month Strategic Outcome
By combining entity authority, local and intent depth, structured machine-readable evidence, AI answer design, and quality-controlled programmatic scale, OPTIME becomes the most citable and trusted source for senior living decisions across traditional and AI search.
