## Objective

Build OPTIME into a trusted decision destination for families searching for senior care, while the recommendation engine continues to develop in parallel.

SEO must never manufacture facts, rankings, ratings, reviews, prices, clinical capabilities, availability, or facility claims. Public pages must distinguish verified facts, source-backed facts, facility-supplied claims, and unknowns.

## Four parallel workstreams

### 1. Website product quality

Audit and improve the complete public journey:
- Homepage
- Guided search
- Results and Top 5 recommendation experience
- Facility detail pages
- Verification request flow
- Comparison experience
- About / methodology / trust pages
- FAQ and educational content
- Mobile usability
- Accessibility
- Performance and Core Web Vitals
- Conversion instrumentation

### 2. Technical SEO foundation

Required baseline:
- Descriptive metadata and canonical URLs
- robots.txt
- XML sitemap
- Search-engine-safe index/noindex policy
- Structured data where factually appropriate
- Open Graph and social metadata
- Stable crawlable URLs
- Internal linking
- Breadcrumbs
- 404 and redirect hygiene
- Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools verification
- Analytics and organic conversion measurement

### 3. Data-backed organic landing pages

Build scalable pages only where OPTIME has sufficient source-backed data and useful unique content.

Priority templates:
- Facility pages
- City pages
- County pages
- Care-need pages
- Rehabilitation / post-hospital care pages
- Comparison pages
- Location + care-need pages when content is genuinely useful and not thin/duplicative

Examples of search intent to serve:
- nursing homes in Miami
- skilled nursing facilities in Miami-Dade
- stroke rehabilitation nursing homes in Miami
- nursing home inspection history
- nursing home staffing and Medicare quality information
- compare nursing homes in a specific market

Do not auto-publish thin combinations merely to create indexable pages.

### 4. Editorial authority

Create family-first guides based on authoritative sources and OPTIME methodology:
- How to choose a nursing home after hospital discharge
- What CMS ratings do and do not tell families
- Questions to ask a skilled nursing facility
- How staffing levels affect care
- Understanding inspections, deficiencies, penalties, and complaints
- Stroke rehabilitation and post-acute care selection
- Medicare / Medicaid educational explainers with clear source dates

Medical, legal, financial, and regulatory content must be source-backed and reviewed for freshness.

## Permanent SEO guardrails

1. Never create fake editorial rankings to target a keyword.
2. Never let a facility pay for organic ranking.
3. Sponsored/commercial placement must be clearly separated from organic recommendation logic.
4. Unknown data remains unknown; do not fill SEO copy with inferred claims.
5. Do not publish thousands of thin near-duplicate location pages.
6. Public claims must have provenance and freshness where relevant.
7. Results pages containing personalized/private query state should not become indexable SEO landing pages.
8. SEO work runs in parallel with recommendation-engine work and must not silently alter ranking semantics.
