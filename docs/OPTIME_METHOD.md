# The OPTIME Method

## Mission
OPTIME does not answer questions.

OPTIME builds expertise.

Every answer is the visible result of years of accumulated knowledge, continuous research, and objective reasoning.

## The OPTIME Method
People trust experts.

Experts are not trusted because they speak well.

Experts are trusted because they know.

Therefore OPTIME must first become the world's best expert.

Only then become the world's best advisor.

## The Expert Mind
Before every recommendation the Institute asks:

- What is happening?
- Why is it happening?
- What evidence supports this?
- What contradicts it?
- What matters most?
- What matters least?
- What would an experienced specialist notice?
- What would the family probably overlook?
