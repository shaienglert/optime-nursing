# Combined Care Solution Contract

OPTIME ranks complete resident solutions, not facilities in isolation.

A care MUST may be satisfied by:

1. verified in-house care, or
2. verified permission for outside care **plus** a verified external-care agency match that covers the required services in the facility's service area.

Outside-care permission by itself is never a PASS. It only keeps an otherwise attractive housing option alive as `PENDING_VERIFICATION` until the agency layer closes the gap.

This is especially material when the resident prefers an intimate/home-like or independent setting, or when the ADL need is temporary/part-time after surgery or hospitalization.

The agency universe must provide a canonical agency identity, verification status, service-area match, service coverage, minimum-hours policy, price evidence when known, and source/provenance. UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN.
