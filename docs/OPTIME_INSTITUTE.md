## The Knowledge Standard
Knowledge is accepted only after:

- Collection
- Normalization
- Verification
- Evidence Review
- Conflict Resolution
- Publication

Knowledge is never accepted because an AI suggested it.

Every published fact must answer:
- Where did this information come from?
- What is the fact?
- Why is it true?
- Who supports it?
- How recent is it?
- Who verified it?
- How strong is the evidence?
- Is there conflicting evidence?
- When should it be reviewed again?
