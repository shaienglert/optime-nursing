# OPTIME Mission

## Primary Mission
Build and maintain a complete,
verified,
and continuously updated database
of every eligible Senior Living community
in Florida.

OPTIME exists for one purpose:

To become the world's most trusted independent authority on Senior Living decisions.

We are not building an AI.

We are building an Institution.

An institution whose knowledge grows every day.

One that families, hospitals, physicians, social workers, and discharge planners trust.

## Our Constitution

- Knowledge before Algorithms.
- Evidence before Opinions.
- Truth before Marketing.
- Resident before Provider.
- Family before Revenue.
- Transparency before Certainty.
- Learning before Scaling.

## Our Purpose

Every day OPTIME should know more than it knew yesterday.

Not because a language model became smarter.

Because OPTIME itself became smarter.

## The Core Asset

The most valuable asset of OPTIME is not AI.

It is institutional knowledge.
