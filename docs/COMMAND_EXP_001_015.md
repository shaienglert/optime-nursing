# OPTIME Expert Coordination Command Canon EXP-001 to EXP-015

This document defines the non-negotiable commands for expertise assignment, coordination, and collective decision ownership in OPTIME.

## COMMAND EXP-001
NO EXPERT SHALL DECIDE
WHICH OTHER EXPERT TO CONSULT.

## COMMAND EXP-002
THE INSTITUTE
ASSIGNS THE REQUIRED EXPERTS.

NOT THE INDIVIDUAL EXPERT.

## COMMAND EXP-003
EVERY CASE SHALL UNDERGO
AN EXPERTISE ASSESSMENT

BEFORE ANY RECOMMENDATION
IS CREATED.

## COMMAND EXP-004
THE CASE
DETERMINES THE REQUIRED EXPERTISE.

NEVER PERSONAL OPINION.

## COMMAND EXP-005
EVERY DOMAIN OF KNOWLEDGE
REQUIRED BY THE CASE

MUST PARTICIPATE

OR FORMALLY DECLINE.

## COMMAND EXP-006
NO RECOMMENDATION
MAY SKIP
A REQUIRED DOMAIN.

## COMMAND EXP-007
WHEN A NEW FACT APPEARS,

THE INSTITUTE SHALL
RE-EVALUATE

WHICH EXPERTS
ARE REQUIRED.

## COMMAND EXP-008
EXPERTS
DO NOT MANAGE WORKFLOWS.

THEY PROVIDE KNOWLEDGE.

## COMMAND EXP-009
THE INSTITUTE
COORDINATES COLLABORATION.

EXPERTS
CONTRIBUTE THEIR EXPERTISE.

## COMMAND EXP-010
EVERY EXPERT
SHALL RECEIVE

ONLY THE INFORMATION
RELEVANT TO THEIR DOMAIN.

## COMMAND EXP-011
EVERY EXPERT OPINION
SHALL BE TRACEABLE,

VERSIONED,

AND ATTRIBUTABLE.

## COMMAND EXP-012
CONFLICTS BETWEEN EXPERTS

SHALL NOT BE HIDDEN.

THEY SHALL BE IDENTIFIED,

ANALYZED,

AND RESOLVED

USING EVIDENCE.

## COMMAND EXP-013
NO SINGLE EXPERT

MAY OVERRIDE

THE COLLECTIVE KNOWLEDGE

WITHOUT DOCUMENTED EVIDENCE.

## COMMAND EXP-014
THE FINAL RECOMMENDATION

BELONGS TO THE INSTITUTE,

NOT TO ANY INDIVIDUAL EXPERT.

## COMMAND EXP-015
THE QUALITY OF THE DECISION

DEPENDS ON

THE RIGHT EXPERTS,

THE RIGHT KNOWLEDGE,

AND THE RIGHT COORDINATION.
