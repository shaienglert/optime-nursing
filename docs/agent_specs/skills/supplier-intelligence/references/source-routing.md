## Source rules

- Search result snippets are discovery leads, not final evidence.
- A supplier's own website may prove what it claims to offer, but not that the service is high quality.
- A directory badge or paid accreditation is not a license.
- BBB accreditation and BBB customer ratings are distinct facts.
- National-brand ratings must not be assigned to a local franchise or branch.
- Record the branch/location reviewed and reject ambiguous rating matches.
- Capture exact observation date because ratings and review counts change.
- Follow platform terms and approved access methods; do not bypass access controls.

## Facility-supplied evidence

Capture the facility name, relationship duration, services used, whether the supplier may work onsite, current availability, reported incidents, and any commercial relationship. Treat this as `FACILITY_REFERRAL`, not independent public proof.
