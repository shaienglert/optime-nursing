# OPTIME Vision

## Mission
Help families and professionals make senior living decisions with the confidence of a world-class independent advisor.

## Institutional Vision
OPTIME becomes a continuously learning knowledge institution, not a transactional marketplace.

The platform grows through:
- verified knowledge
- structured evidence
- provider intelligence
- knowledge graphs
- outcome learning
- professional reasoning

## Core Platform
The OPTIME platform combines six layers:
- Resident Understanding
- Recommendation Decision Engine
- Prepared Intelligence
- Knowledge Repository
- Expert Knowledge Workforce
- Narrative Advisor

## Formula
Recommendation Quality = Verified Knowledge x Resident Fit x Outcome Awareness x Clear Explanation

## Vertical #1
Senior Living

## Future Verticals
- Senior Living remains the proving ground for the institutional knowledge model.
- Future expansion only happens after OPTIME establishes trusted authority in this domain.
