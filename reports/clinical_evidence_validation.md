## Clinical Explanations and Evidence References

- Speech therapy is important after stroke because communication recovery may improve independence and participation in daily life.
  - Capability: Speech Therapy
  - Evidence: EV-001 (High)
- Support for safe swallowing may help reduce avoidable complications for residents with swallowing concerns.
  - Capability: Swallow Safety Support
  - Evidence: EV-002 (Moderate), EV-006 (Limited)
- A fall prevention program matters for residents using a walker because it may reduce injury risk.
  - Capability: Walker Accessibility
  - Evidence: EV-003 (High)
- Reliable 24/7 nursing support matters for residents with high day-to-day care needs.
  - Capability: 24/7 Nursing
  - Evidence: EV-009 (High), EV-010 (Moderate)
- Gluten-free meal accommodation helps align daily nutrition support with the resident preference and restriction.
  - Capability: Gluten-Free Meals
  - Evidence: EV-007 (Moderate)
- Meaningful activities such as movies and music may support social engagement and quality of life.
  - Capability: Social Activities
  - Evidence: EV-008 (Moderate)
