# Scientific Method

## Mission
Every Knowledge Center shall operate as a scientific institution.

Knowledge is never merely collected.
Knowledge is discovered, validated, challenged, improved, and preserved.

## Scientific Cycle

- Question
- Research
- Evidence Collection
- Critical Review
- Verification
- Knowledge Object
- Expert Review
- Institutional Knowledge
- Recommendation
- Outcome
- Validation
- Knowledge Improvement

## Governing Rules

- No research without a clearly defined research question.
- No knowledge without explicit evidence, evidence strength, evidence quality, supporting sources, conflicting evidence, and knowledge gaps.
- No single study may change institutional knowledge on its own.
- Every agent must ask what is known, how it is known, what supports it, what contradicts it, and what is still missing.
