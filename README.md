# OPTIME Nursing Mission

Our goal is to become the Booking.com of Senior Living.

Families do not need the highest rated facility.

They need the facility that is the best fit for their loved one.

OPTIME combines:

- Quality data
- Staffing levels
- Safety indicators
- Family reviews
- Price
- Personal preferences

to generate an OPTIME Score and an OPTIME Fit Score.
